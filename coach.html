Coach Hairston

Dear friend of Berkmar,

I'm Reggie Hairston, Head Girls Basketball Coach at Berkmar High School. I invite you to invest in our student-athletes — on and off the court.

Our program is built on excellence, character, teamwork, and academic success. Through basketball, these young women learn lifelong lessons in leadership and perseverance that prepare them for the future.

As we prepare for the season, we're seeking community partners to help provide uniforms, equipment, tournament fees, travel, training, team meals, and end-of-season recognition. Every gift, large or small, makes a difference.

Reggie Hairston
Head Girls Basketball Coach · Berkmar High School
