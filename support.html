Ways to Support

The Lady Patriots Tip-Off Club is an independent, community- and parent-led nonprofit supporting Berkmar High School Girls Basketball — varsity and JV.

In-Kind Donations

Prefer to give goods or services? We gladly accept team meals, snacks & water, printing, transportation, equipment, gift cards, banners, and professional services. In-kind gifts receive recognition equal to their value at the matching sponsorship level.
